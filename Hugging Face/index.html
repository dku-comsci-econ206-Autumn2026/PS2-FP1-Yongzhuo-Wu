Can correction services reduce AI sycophancy?

Two apps choose verifiable correction (H) or sycophancy (S). Each H app commits one identical correction slot. Three users each want at most one slot. All model values use hypothetical common payoff units.

Evidence status: exact model calculations and one retained author self-reflection (n=1). No observed purchases, population demand estimates, live LLM outputs or measured learning effects.

1. Compare incentives and costs

Apps choose simultaneously with complete payoff information and unobserved actions. After supply k is fixed, the top k sealed bids win and each pays the highest losing bid. Lower user ID breaks ties. There is one round, no reserve or loser payment. Each H app receives one payment minus delivery cost.

Why cost 1 changes the result

Strong demand gives the H-minus-S gain 1−c against either opponent action. At c=1, A's two payoff rows coincide and B's two payoff columns coincide. Each player is indifferent against every opponent mixture. Every pair of independent mixed strategies is a Nash equilibrium. Four pure profiles are only the corners of this continuous set. Extra revenue changes behavior only through net payment.

5. Understand the limits and proposed test


An accuracy mechanism predicts choosing correction and improved delayed factual recall. An obvious error, project cues, and response wording can also explain this choice. A discriminating design would match tone and randomize display order across several checkable items, then measure delayed accuracy separately from liking and compare stated WTP with an incentivized purchase. No participant study is scheduled here.


Practical case: limited tutoring correction capacity. Providers would disclose cost and verifiable delivery, educators would assess learning, public institutions could support basic correction and independent reviewers would audit errors and hear appeals. Paid access can exclude low-budget learners and reward providers for avoidable errors. Free basic correction, subsidies, a correction log and minority input are proposed protections, not deployed features. SDG 4 learning/access effects remain unmeasured.


Truthful bidding assumes fixed supply, identical slots, private unit demand and quasilinear utility. Variable quality, common values, budgets, collusion and post-bid supply need a different model. The current matrices do not evaluate actual AI output or real firms.
